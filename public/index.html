FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Reservation & Hospitality Policies

How does Maison Aurélia prevent double bookings?

Every room selection places an immediate expiring database hold (10 minutes) backed by SQLite BEGIN IMMEDIATE serialised transactions and database-level exclusion triggers. Even if two guests click confirm at the same millisecond, only one transaction succeeds.

What are the check-in, check-out, and same-day turnover rules?

Check-in begins at 15:00 and check-out is until 12:00 noon local hotel time. Under our standard turnover policy, a departure date is available for an incoming guest arriving that afternoon.

What payment options and deposit plans are accepted?

Guests may choose between paying 100% in full or a 30% booking deposit online via tokenised credit/debit card, verified bank wire transfer, or authorised Payment-at-Hotel guarantee.

How do cancellations and automatic refunds work?

Cancellations made more than 48 hours before arrival receive a 100% automatic refund to the original payment method and immediately release the physical room back into live inventory.
